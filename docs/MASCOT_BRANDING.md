# Mascot & Branding: CodeMind CLI

## 1. Brand Mark

The authoritative brand mark for CodeMind CLI is:

```text
▟●▙ CodeMind
```

- Left block (`▟`): Solid geometric foundation.
- Center dot (`●`): Active neural core / Milo focus indicator.
- Right block (`▙`): Structural balance.
- Typeface: Clean monospaced or sans-serif typography.

---

## 2. Mascot: Milo

**Milo** is the working name for CodeMind's autonomous engineering companion.
- **Personality:** Calm, methodical, thoughtful, collaborative, and honest.
- **Behavior:** Milo never claims unverified success, asks for confirmation on dangerous maneuvers, and explains complex architectures with clarity.
- **Color Accent:** Restrained warm amber / orange (`#FF9E3B`).
